## 14.3 The assumptions that must be validated before starting the company

1. **Outcome-based willingness to pay.** MSMEs (or their CAs) will pay monthly or success fees tied to rupees recovered, orders won, hours saved or penalties avoided. *Test:* paid pilots with deposits, not stated intent.
2. **Channel leverage.** CA firms and cluster associations deliver customers at ≤ ₹50k CAC (≤ ₹1 lakh for O6) and actively refer. *Test:* 2 CA sessions + 2 association demos → pilots per event.
3. **Trust in AI acting on the owner's behalf.** Owners allow an AI agent to message their debtors and buyers under their name and language. *Test:* O1 concierge and O3 agent pilots; share of owners who approve autopilot after 2 weeks.
4. **AI accuracy at MSME data quality.** At least 85–90% extraction and response accuracy from phone photos and mixed-language chats, with human review, at a cost that keeps ≥ 65% gross margin. *Test:* O2 Wizard-of-Oz with real CA data; measure acceptance rate and cost per document.
5. **(For O6) Durable regulatory pull.** EU importers keep requiring verified supplier data, and default-value penalties persist as CBAM scope widens. *Test:* interviews with 5+ EU importers or their Indian sourcing offices, and 3 verifiers.

**If assumptions 1 and 2 fail across opportunities**, the MSME software thesis itself is in doubt for this team. Pivot toward capital-backed models (O4, O5) where MSMEs pay through margins and savings rather than subscriptions.
