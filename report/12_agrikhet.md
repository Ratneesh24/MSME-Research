## 12.3 The Track D thesis: an MSME decarbonisation stack

```
  MEASURE                         REDUCE                              MONETISE
  O6 CBAM / product carbon   ──►  O5 rooftop solar + efficiency  ──►  lower CBAM cost for exporters
  footprint for exporters &       Agri-residue biomass fuel for       green loans / sustainability-linked credit
  their suppliers                 MSME boilers (Ecoharvest link)      carbon credits (later; integrity-sensitive)
```

**Why it hangs together:**
1. O6 earns early, recurring software revenue from an *urgent* regulatory need with identifiable buyers (3–4k direct + 25–30k indirect exporters).
2. The O6 data shows *where* emissions sit (power, fuel, process), which directly generates O5 and biomass project leads.
3. Abatement reduces CBAM liability, so the exporter has a hard rupee reason to act.
4. The **agri-residue link** (crop residue → briquettes/pellets → MSME boilers) fits the AgriKhet/Ecoharvest name. It also addresses residue burning, a recurring national problem.

**What is NOT yet evidenced (must be validated before building):**
- prices, supply reliability and seasonality of biomass fuel in target clusters;
- MSME boiler fuel-switch economics (capex for boiler modification, fuel-cost delta);
- whether MSMEs will sign multi-year fuel contracts.

**No numbers are asserted here because none were retrieved.**

**Carbon-credit caution.** India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme and voluntary markets exist, but MSME-level credits are small and verification-intensive, and integrity scrutiny is rising. Treat carbon revenue as **upside, not the base case**.

## 12.4 Recommendation for AgriKhet

1. **Run two validation tracks in parallel in the 30-day plan (chapter 13):**
   - **Track D lead: O6 (CBAM/carbon)** in Ludhiana/Mandi Gobindgarh steel clusters. It fits the brand and the evidence, needs little capital, and earns revenue quickly.
   - **Track A/E lead: O1 + O2** (receivables + CA copilot) in Rajkot/Ahmedabad. It has the strongest evidence and the largest scalable software market. Use a separate product brand.
2. **Do not start with Track C (FPOs)** until FPO willingness to pay, or a programme-funded buyer, is evidenced.
3. **Keep Track B as an expansion path.** Agri-processing clusters can later adopt O1/O4/O5 once the core product works.
4. **Build the unfair advantage deliberately.** It does not exist today. In 90 days, aim for:
   - one cluster-association MoU (e.g., AIIFA or a Ludhiana association);
   - 10 CA-firm design partners;
   - one verifier/consultant partnership for CBAM.
