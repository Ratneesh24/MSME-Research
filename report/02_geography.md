## 2.4 District level: what the first-ever NSO district estimates show (Sep 2026)

The NSO released ASUSE 2025 estimates for **757 districts** (S02). Key facts:

- The **top 10 districts hold ~10%** of establishments, workers and GVA. The **top 50 districts (in 12 states) hold about one-third**.
- Only **16 districts exceed 5 lakh establishments**: 8 in West Bengal, 3 Maharashtra, 2 Gujarat, 1 each in Karnataka, Telangana and UP.
- **North 24 Parganas** is #1 (16.59 lakh establishments, 21.3 lakh workers), then **South 24 Parganas** (10.29 lakh; 12.4 lakh workers), **Surat** (8.51 lakh), **Ahmedabad** (7.30 lakh), **Rangareddy** (6.93 lakh), **Prayagraj** (6.32 lakh), **Pune** (6.01 lakh) and **Bengaluru Urban** (~5 lakh).
- **Productivity varies 2.7×.** GVA per worker ranges from about ₹1.0 lakh (Murshidabad) to ₹2.72 lakh (Rangareddy). 280 districts are above the national ₹1,56,539, and 331 sit between ₹1.0 and 1.5 lakh. Emoluments per hired worker range from about ₹80k in parts of Bihar and Jharkhand to ₹4.64 lakh in Dehradun.

**Important nuance.** The districts with the *most* establishments (24 Parganas, Prayagraj, Murshidabad) are dominated by low-productivity own-account trade and services. The districts with the highest *ability to pay* are the productive industrial ones: Pune, Rangareddy/Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Coimbatore, Chennai, Ludhiana, Gurugram–Faridabad. **Rank target districts by count × productivity × B2B intensity, not by count alone.**

## 2.5 Clusters: the dataset

`data/clusters.csv` lists **142 named MSME clusters** across 25 States/UTs. Fields: state, district, cluster/town, sector, major products, type (industrial/export/artisanal), export relevance and the opportunities each cluster suits. Unit counts per cluster were **not retrieved** and are marked "data unavailable" rather than guessed.

Programme context: 606 MSE-CDP cluster projects approved (364 completed); ODOP covers 1,102 products in 761 districts; FMC's mapping identified ~6,600 clusters, most of them artisanal (S59).
