Brief §30–31, §35 · Before building

Two theses to test, and five assumptions that decide them

MSME finance back-office

O2 CA copilot → O1 receivables copilot → O15 credit access. Win CA firms first; they hold the data and the trust for crores of MSMEs. Test in Rajkot and Ahmedabad.

MSME decarbonisation stack

O6 CBAM/carbon compliance → O5 energy-as-a-service → agri-residue biomass fuel. This fits AgriKhet's Ecoharvest identity. Test in Ludhiana and Mandi Gobindgarh steel clusters.

  1. Outcome-based willingness to pay. MSMEs or their CAs pay monthly or success fees tied to rupees recovered, orders won or penalties avoided. The test is paid pilots with deposits.
  2. Channel leverage. CA firms and cluster associations deliver customers at ₹50,000 CAC or less, and refer actively.
  3. Trust in an AI agent. Owners let an agent message their debtors and buyers, in their name and language.
  4. Accuracy at MSME data quality. 85–90% extraction accuracy on phone photos and mixed-language chats, with gross margin of 65% or more.
  5. CBAM pull persists. EU importers keep demanding verified supplier emissions data.

Kill rule: stop an opportunity if fewer than 3 of 20 target firms pay after seeing results, or if CAC exceeds 12 months of gross profit.
