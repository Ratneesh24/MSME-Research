# MSME owner interview guide (45 minutes)

**Purpose:** find out which problems MSME owners actually pay to solve. Use *past behaviour* ("the last time…"), not opinions about hypothetical products.
**Languages:** conduct the interview in the owner's language. Have a translated copy ready (Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Punjabi).
**Consent:** read the consent script. Do not record without explicit consent. Store notes without personal phone numbers or emails (DPDP Act).

---

## 0. Consent script (1 min)
"We are researching problems faced by businesses like yours. This is not a sales call. The interview takes about 45 minutes. We will not share your name or business details. May we take notes, and may we record audio for our own use only?" → record Yes/No.

## 1. Business profile (5 min) → coding sheet columns A–J
1. What does your business make, sell or do? Main products and customers (B2B/B2C, government, export).
2. Approximate annual turnover band: < ₹40 lakh / 40 lakh–1.5 cr / 1.5–10 cr / 10–100 cr / > 100 cr.
3. Number of employees (permanent / contract).
4. Udyam registered? GST (regular/composition)? E-invoicing? Export (IEC)?
5. Who handles accounts: in-house accountant, CA, both?
6. Who makes purchasing decisions for software and services? (owner / partner / family member / manager) **[Brief Q19]**

## 2. Problems: unaided first (10 min) **[Brief Q1–Q6]**
7. What is your biggest business problem right now? *(Do not prompt.)* **[Q1]**
8. What costs you the most money? **[Q2]**
9. What consumes the most of *your* time each week? **[Q3]**
10. What causes you the most stress? **[Q4]**
11. Tell me about the last customer you lost. Why did you lose them? **[Q5]**
12. Where do you lose revenue: unpaid invoices, lost leads, rejections, price swings, something else? **[Q6]**

*Then aided:* show a card with 12 problems (receivables, credit, finding customers, following up leads, compliance/GST, labour compliance, raw-material prices, suppliers, logistics, energy cost, skilled workers, export/CBAM). Ask: "Rank your top 3. For each, how often does it hit you (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly), and roughly how many ₹ or hours does it cost per month?"

## 3. Current spending and tools (8 min) **[Q7–Q11, Q20]**
13. What do you currently pay for? (CA fees, Tally/software, IndiaMART/Justdial, consultants, lawyers, collection agents, freight brokers, energy auditors). Approximate monthly or annual amounts. **[Q7]**
14. Which software do you use today? Who uses it? **[Q8]**
15. Which software did you stop using, and why? **[Q9]**
16. What is still done on Excel, paper or WhatsApp? Show me one example if possible. **[Q10]**
17. How do you currently find new customers? What share comes from referrals, dealers, IndiaMART/Justdial, exhibitions, exports, government tenders? **[Q20]**
18. If you could hand one task to someone (or something) to automate tomorrow, what would it be? **[Q11]**

## 4. Problem deep-dives (12 min): ask only the modules relevant to the respondent

**Module R – Receivables (O1)**
- How many customers owe you money right now? How much is overdue beyond 60 days? Beyond 180 days?
- Walk me through the last time you chased a late payment. Who called? How many times? What happened?
- Have you ever charged interest, sent a legal notice, used MSME Samadhaan/MSEFC or TReDS? Why or why not?
- Would you allow a service to send reminders to your customers on your behalf, in their language? What would worry you?

**Module C – CA firms (O2)** *(for CA respondents)*
- How do clients send you documents? How many hours per client per month go on data entry and reconciliation?
- What software do you pay for today, and how much a year?
- What happens in the last 5 days before GST due dates?
- Would you trust AI-drafted Tally vouchers if your staff approved them? What accuracy would you need?

**Module L – Leads and sales (O3)**
- How many enquiries do you get a month, from which sources? How quickly do you reply, and who replies?
- Of 100 enquiries, how many become quotes, and how many become orders?
- What do you pay for leads per year? Is it worth it? Why?

**Module E – Exports and CBAM (O6)**
- Do you export to the EU (directly or as a supplier)? What has your buyer asked for since January 2026?
- Have you been asked for emissions data or been charged default values? What did it cost?
- Who prepares this today (consultant, in-house, buyer)? At what cost?

## 5. Willingness to pay (5 min) **[Q12–Q18]**
19. Which problem would you pay **₹500/month** to solve? **₹2,000/month? ₹5,000? ₹10,000? ₹25,000?** **[Q12–Q16]**
    - Record the highest price point accepted for each named problem.
20. Van Westendorp for the top problem: at what monthly price would the solution be *too cheap to trust*, *a bargain*, *getting expensive*, *too expensive to consider*?
21. Would you prefer a monthly fee, a fee only when it works (success fee), or a one-time fee?
22. What would you **never** pay for? **[Q17]**
23. What stops you from adopting software? (cost, staff skill, trust/data, past bad experience, language, "my CA handles it") **[Q18]**

## 6. Close (2 min)
- "Can we show you a prototype in 2 weeks? Would you be willing to try it with your real data for 30 days?" → design-partner flag.
- Referral: "Who else in your association should we speak to?"

---

### Interviewer notes
- Ask for **specific recent examples**, with numbers.
- Never pitch before section 5.
- Score each problem's severity 1–10 *from the evidence the respondent gives*, not from their adjectives.
- Fill `response_coding_template.csv` within 2 hours of the interview.
