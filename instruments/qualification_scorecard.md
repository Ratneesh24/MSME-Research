# Design-partner qualification scorecard

Score each criterion 0 / 1 / 2. A **design partner** needs a total of **≥ 9 / 12** and must score 2 on "Pain evidence" and "Already pays".

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit with ICP (size, sector, cluster) | Outside ICP | Adjacent | Exact ICP |
| Pain evidence | Opinion only | Example without numbers | Recent example with ₹/hours |
| Frequency | Yearly or less | Monthly | Weekly or daily |
| Already pays for a workaround | No | Small/indirect (staff time) | Yes, ₹ (CA, leads, lawyer, consultant) |
| Decision-maker access | No access | Influencer | Owner/partner engaged |
| Data access for pilot | Refuses | Partial | Will share Tally/WhatsApp/bills |

**Opportunity-specific must-haves**
- **O1:** ≥ 30 debtors and ≥ ₹50 lakh overdue > 60 days; Tally or exportable ledger.
- **O2:** CA firm with ≥ 5 staff and ≥ 100 GST clients; Tally-based.
- **O3:** pays for leads; ≥ 50 enquiries/month; maintains a price list.
- **O6:** EU exposure (direct or upstream); 12 months of energy and fuel bills available.

**Red flags (disqualify):** wants it free indefinitely; will not share any data; no owner involvement; expects custom development.
