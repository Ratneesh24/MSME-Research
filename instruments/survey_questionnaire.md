# MSME pain & willingness-to-pay survey (≈12 minutes)

Distribute via association WhatsApp groups and CA firms as a Google Form or WhatsApp flow. Translate into Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Punjabi and Marathi. **No personal data is required.** The optional contact field is used only for pilot invitations, with consent.

**Section A: Profile**
1. Sector: Manufacturing (engineering/auto · textiles/apparel · food/agri-processing · chemicals/pharma · plastics · other) / Trading-distribution / Services (B2B) / Services (B2C)
2. State and district
3. Annual turnover: < ₹40 lakh · ₹40 lakh–1.5 cr · ₹1.5–10 cr · ₹10–100 cr · > ₹100 cr
4. Employees: 1–5 · 6–20 · 21–50 · 51–200 · > 200
5. Registrations (multi-select): Udyam · GST regular · GST composition · e-invoicing · IEC/exporter · GeM seller
6. Who handles accounts? In-house · CA · Both · Nobody formally
7. Who decides on buying software/services? Owner · Partner/family · Manager

**Section B: Problems** (for each of 12 problems: *How often does it affect you?* Never / Yearly / Monthly / Weekly / Daily; *How serious?* 1–10)
- Customers paying late · Getting loans/working capital · Finding new customers · Following up enquiries · GST/accounting work · Labour/PF/ESI compliance · Licences/renewals · Raw-material price changes · Unreliable suppliers · Transport cost · Electricity/fuel cost · Hiring skilled workers · Export paperwork/CBAM (exporters only)

8. Pick your **top 3** problems from the list above.
9. For your #1 problem, roughly how much does it cost you per month? (₹0–10k · 10–50k · 50k–2 lakh · 2–10 lakh · > 10 lakh · don't know)
10. For your #1 problem, how many hours a week do you or your staff spend on it?

**Section C: Current spending and tools**
11. What do you pay for today? (multi-select with ₹/month band): CA · Accounting software · IndiaMART/Justdial/other leads · WhatsApp tools · Consultants · Lawyers/collection agents · Freight brokers · None
12. Tools you use (multi-select): Tally · Busy · Marg · Vyapar · myBillBook · Zoho · Excel only · Paper only · ERP · CRM · WhatsApp Business app · WhatsApp API tool
13. Have you stopped using any software in the last 2 years? Which, and why? (free text)
14. Overdue receivables > 60 days (₹ band) *(B2B sellers only)*
15. Monthly enquiries received; % that become orders *(B2B sellers only)*

**Section D: Willingness to pay**
16. To solve your #1 problem completely, the most you would pay per month: ₹0 · ₹500 · ₹2,000 · ₹5,000 · ₹10,000 · ₹25,000+
17. Preferred pricing: Monthly fee · Pay only on results (success fee) · One-time fee · Through my CA/bank
18. Biggest reason you would NOT adopt a new solution: Cost · Staff can't use it · Data/trust · Language · Bad past experience · My CA does it · Not a priority
19. Would you like to try a free pilot of a solution for your #1 problem? Yes/No. (If yes: business name + WhatsApp, with consent)

**Analysis:** see chapter 13 §13.1. Discount stated WTP by ~50%. Weight results by turnover band when reporting national figures.
